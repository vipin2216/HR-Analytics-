# HR-Analytics-Power BI Dashboard
📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI
This interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built with Power BI provides a comprehensive overview of employee attrition across various dimensions. It is designed to help HR teams and decision-makers identify key attrition trends and areas of concern for strategic workforce planning.

🔍 Key Features:
Overall Stats:

Total Employees: 1,470

Attrition Count: 237

Attrition Rate: 16.1%

Avg. Age: 37 years

Avg. Salary: 6.5K

Avg. Years of Working: 7 years

Visual Insights:

📌 Attrition by Age Group: Highest among 26–35 age group

💼 Attrition by Job Role: Top 5 roles with highest attrition include:

Laboratory Technician

Sales Executive

Research Scientist

Sales Representative

Human Resources

📚 Attrition by Education: Life Sciences & Medical degrees show higher attrition rates

💰 Attrition by Salary Slab: Majority earning up to 5K are leaving

📈 Attrition by Years of Experience: Significant drop after 1–2 years

Employee Sentiment:

Job satisfaction scores are broken down by roles, helping assess engagement

Demographic Trends:

Gender-wise attrition: Male: 150, Female: 87

Power BI (DAX, Data Modeling, Visuals)
